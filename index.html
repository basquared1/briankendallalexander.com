About

Practical counsel, creative-friendly execution.

I am an entertainment Business & Legal Affairs attorney with more than 20 years of experience in the acquisition, development, production and licensing of film and television content.

My background includes senior in-house roles at UMG/Polygram Entertainment, Discovery/TLC and Viacom, as well as leading production legal at Magical Elves. I have worked within studios, networks and production companies, giving me a practical understanding of what creative and production teams need from their lawyers.

Much of that work has been music-driven. I have negotiated master use, sync and publishing rights with labels, publishers, artists and estates, and structured the royalty, participation and approval terms underneath them.

I negotiate and draft development, production, co-production, talent, rights, format, services, licensing and distribution agreements and independently handle production legal and clearance matters throughout the life of a project.

I am also building music rights licensing software: clearance, rate cards, approvals and license generation across master use and publishing, covering both catalog and live music.

How I work

Practical, collaborative and production-minded.

I keep negotiations focused on the business points that matter, with terms that are clear, defensible and workable in production.

My approach is direct, responsive and relationship-focused. I protect the company’s interests while recognizing that a successful deal should also preserve the creative and business relationships needed to make the project.

Selected work

UMG / Polygram Entertainment

From 2017 through 2025, I served as the lead in-house Business & Legal Affairs attorney for UMG’s film and television slate, primarily through Polygram Entertainment. I remained hands-on throughout the full lifecycle of projects, negotiating and drafting agreements and handling production legal, rights and clearance matters from development through delivery. The slate was overwhelmingly music-driven, which meant negotiating master use, sync and publishing rights with labels, publishers, artists and estates on nearly every title.
